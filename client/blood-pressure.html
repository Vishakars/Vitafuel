Blood Pressure Management Tips

Take readings at the same time each day for consistency.
Sit quietly for 5 minutes before taking a reading.
Avoid caffeine, smoking, and exercise 30 minutes before measurement.
Use the correct cuff size and position your arm at heart level.
Maintain a low-sodium diet and regular exercise routine.
Take multiple readings and record the average.
Share your readings with your healthcare provider regularly.
Track patterns and note what activities affect your readings.

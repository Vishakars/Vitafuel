Daily Mental Health Tips

Start your day with 5 minutes of deep breathing to set a positive tone.
Take regular breaks from screens and go for short walks to clear your mind.
Connect with friends and family - social support is vital for mental health.
Keep a gratitude journal - write down 3 things you're grateful for each day.
Establish a consistent sleep routine for better emotional regulation.
Listen to calming music or nature sounds when feeling overwhelmed.
Practice mindfulness by focusing on the present moment throughout your day.

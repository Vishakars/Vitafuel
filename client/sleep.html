Sleep Tips & Science

Temperature Matters: Your body temperature naturally drops before sleep. Keep your bedroom between 16-19°C (60-67°F) for optimal sleep quality.
Blue Light Blues: Blue light from screens suppresses melatonin production. Use blue light filters or avoid screens 1-2 hours before bed.
The 3-2-1 Rule: Stop eating 3 hours before bed, stop drinking 2 hours before, and stop screens 1 hour before for better sleep.
Consistent Schedule: Going to bed and waking up at the same time daily helps regulate your circadian rhythm, even on weekends.
Morning Sunlight: Get 10-15 minutes of morning sunlight to help regulate your sleep-wake cycle naturally.
Caffeine Half-Life: Caffeine stays in your system for 6-8 hours. Avoid coffee after 2 PM if you sleep at 10 PM.

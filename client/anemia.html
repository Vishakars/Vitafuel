Enhanced Anemia Management Tips

Eat iron-rich foods like spinach, red meat, beans, and fortified cereals daily.
Combine iron-rich foods with vitamin C sources (citrus fruits, bell peppers) to enhance absorption.
Avoid tea, coffee, or milk with iron-rich meals as they can inhibit absorption.
Take iron supplements on an empty stomach for better absorption.
Regular blood tests help monitor your hemoglobin and ferritin levels.
Stay hydrated to support healthy blood circulation.
Light exercise helps improve circulation, but rest when fatigued.
Ensure 7–9 hours of sleep for recovery.
Keep a consistent supplement schedule.

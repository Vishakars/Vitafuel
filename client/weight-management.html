Weight Management Tips & Reminders

Focus on balanced meals with protein, veggies, and whole grains rather than restrictive dieting.
Drink a glass of water before meals — it helps improve digestion and prevent overeating.
Aim for at least 30 minutes of physical activity daily — walking counts!
Weigh yourself at the same time each day for accurate tracking.
Prioritize good sleep — poor sleep affects hunger hormones and metabolism.
Eat slowly — it takes 20 minutes for your brain to register fullness.
Choose whole, unprocessed foods when possible — they keep you full longer.
Avoid skipping meals — it may lead to cravings and overeating later.
Build strength — increasing muscle helps boost metabolism even at rest.
Celebrate progress — even small improvements count. Consistency beats perfection.

Diabetes Management Tips

Monitor Regularly: Check your blood glucose at consistent times - before meals, 2 hours after eating, and at bedtime for the best insights.
Meal Planning: Keep a consistent meal schedule and note how different foods affect your glucose levels.
Stay Hydrated: Drink plenty of water throughout the day to help maintain stable blood sugar levels.
Exercise Smart: Regular physical activity helps improve insulin sensitivity - even a 10-minute walk after meals can help!
Healthcare Team: Share your glucose logs with your healthcare provider to optimize your treatment plan.
Quality Sleep: Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep, as poor sleep can affect blood sugar control.

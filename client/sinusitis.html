Treatment Tips

Nasal Irrigation: Use saline nasal rinses 2-3 times daily to flush out irritants and mucus. Use distilled or boiled water for safety.
Warm Compresses: Apply warm, moist compresses to affected areas for 10-15 minutes to reduce pain and promote drainage.
Stay Hydrated: Drink plenty of water to thin mucus secretions and support your immune system.
Sleep Position: Sleep with your head elevated using extra pillows to promote sinus drainage during the night.
Humidity Control: Use a humidifier to maintain 40-50% humidity. Clean it regularly to prevent mold growth.
Avoid Irritants: Stay away from smoke, strong perfumes, cleaning chemicals, and other known triggers.
Manage Stress: Practice stress-reduction techniques as stress can worsen sinus symptoms.
Anti-inflammatory Foods: Include foods rich in omega-3s, vitamin C, and antioxidants in your diet.

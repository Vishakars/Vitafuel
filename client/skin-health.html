Skin Care Tips & Reminders

Always cleanse your face twice daily - morning and evening to remove dirt, oil, and makeup.
Apply sunscreen every day, even indoors or on cloudy days - UV rays penetrate windows.
Introduce new products gradually (one at a time) to avoid irritation.
Stay hydrated - drink at least 8 glasses of water daily for healthy, plump skin.
Get adequate sleep (7-9 hours) - your skin repairs and regenerates overnight.
Avoid touching your face frequently to reduce breakouts.
Eat a balanced diet rich in antioxidants, vitamins C & E, and omega-3 fatty acids.
Manage stress through meditation, exercise, or hobbies - stress can trigger breakouts.
Change your pillowcase regularly (2-3 times per week) to prevent bacteria buildup.
Use lukewarm water when cleansing - hot water strips natural oils.

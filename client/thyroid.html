Thyroid Health Insights

Maintain a balanced diet with selenium, zinc, and iodine to support thyroid function.
Take thyroid medication at the same time daily, ideally on an empty stomach.
Avoid coffee or high-fiber foods for at least 30 minutes after taking medication.
Monitor heart rate regularly — sudden changes may signal hormone imbalance.
Track weight fluctuations, as they can indicate changes in thyroid activity.
Prioritize sleep — good rest helps regulate hormones and energy levels.
Manage stress through mindfulness or yoga to reduce thyroid-related fatigue.
Review lab results with your doctor regularly to adjust treatment as needed.
